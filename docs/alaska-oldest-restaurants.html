Alaska's oldest restaurants

Restaurants and roadhouses that have fed Alaska from the same spot since 1970 or before: 4 places in all, oldest first. The oldest, Manley Roadhouse in Manley Hot Springs, goes back to 1925.

Which year counts

We count from the year a place opened at its current spot in its current form, going by the place's own history or a published one. A roadhouse moved across a slough counts from the move.

Other guides: Halibut, salmon & crab, Anchorage's global kitchens, James Beard honors, Anchorage restaurant inspections. City pages: Anchorage.

Oldest first

  1. Manley Roadhouse

    100 Front St, Manley Hot Springs

    Roadhouse On the menu: hamburgers, homemade ice cream · Since 1925 · Season: Summer: June 5 to September 27, Friday to Tuesday

    Family-style dining in the tradition of Alaska's old roadhouses, at the end of the Elliott Highway (its own site).

    Its own history page: opened in 1903 as Sam's Meals and Rooms across the slough, and moved to this spot in 1925.

    manleyroadhouse.com

  2. Lucky Wishbone

    1033 E 5th Ave, Anchorage

    America's Classic On the menu: fried chicken, burgers, milkshakes · Since 1955

    Fried chicken, burgers and malts downtown; a 2025 James Beard America's Classic (its own site).

    Its own site: “Located in historic downtown Anchorage, the Lucky Wishbone has served Alaskans … since 1955.”

    luckywishbonealaska.com

  3. The Chart Room Restaurant

    4786 Homer Spit Rd, Homer

    Halibut, salmon & crab On the menu: king crab legs, halibut, fish and chips, oysters, clam chowder · Since 1958

    At Lands End, the tip of the Homer Spit; its specialty is “Halibut Iliamna” (ALASKA.ORG).

    ALASKA.ORG: “a fixture at the end of the famous Homer Spit since 1958.”

  4. Double Musky Inn

    377 Crow Creek Rd, Girdwood

    On the menu: Cajun cooking · Since 1962 · Season: Closed in fall; reopening December 10, 2026

    A Cajun roadhouse on Crow Creek Road in Girdwood; it closes each fall and reopens for winter (its own site).

    Its own site: “Double Musky Inn · Cajun Cuisine · Girdwood, Alaska · Since 1962.”

    Anchorage Health Department inspection: 100/100 on August 23, 2024 (next regular inspection within 12 months)

    doublemuskyinn.com
